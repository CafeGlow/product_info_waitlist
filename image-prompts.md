**ImageAlt text:** "A warm cup of Jagave coffee in a quiet morning setting"  
**Recommended dimensions:** 800 × 1000 px (portrait)

## Notes for Image Generation

- All images should be **photographic** style (not illustrations or 3D renders) to maintain brand consistency
- Avoid showing any text, logos, or branding on products unless explicitly part of the JAGAVE brand
- Maintain a consistent **mood**: clean, scientific, luxurious, warm, and wellness-focused
- Consider generating a few variations of each prompt and selecting the best results
- Images will need to be saved with descriptive filenames (e.g., `hero-coffee-morning.jpg`, `ingredient-arabica.jpg`, etc.)
