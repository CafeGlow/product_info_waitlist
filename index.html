— 04 / Ritual

Four minutes, four steps,
every morning.

The Hikari ritual is engineered for slowness. Four minutes from kettle to cup — enough time to set an intention, not so long that you skip it. Here's how we make it.

— Step 01

Heat water to 92°.

Bring filtered water to a rolling boil, then rest for ninety seconds until it lands at 92°C. Too hot and you'll burn the matcha. Too cool and the collagen won't dissolve. A kitchen thermometer is worth the ten dollars.
↗ 92°C · 220 ml · 90-second rest
— Step 02

Measure 24 grams of First Batch.

Two level scoops. The tin ships with a 12g scoop — the dose is calibrated for a 220ml cup. Don't eyeball it. The functional compounds are dosed to the gram, and so is the caffeine.
↗ 24g · 2 scoops · 92mg caffeine
— Step 03

Whisk in slow circles.

Pour the water in two stages — first 60ml to bloom, whisk in slow figure-eights for fifteen seconds, then the remaining 160ml in a steady stream. A bamboo chasen is traditional; a milk frother works fine. Avoid vigorous beating — it denatures the collagen.
↗ 60ml bloom · 15s whisk · 160ml pour
— Step 04

Wait four minutes. Then drink.

Let the cup rest. The L-theanine needs four minutes to fully bind with the caffeine — this is the difference between a focused morning and a jittery one. Use the time. Look out a window. Set an intention. Then drink slowly, over the next twenty minutes.
↗ 4-minute rest · 20-minute drink · 4-hour plateau
